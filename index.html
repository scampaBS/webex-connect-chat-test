Let's test your chat.

This page hosts the Webex Connect chat widget. Look for the chat launcher in the bottom-right corner to start a conversation.

Quick test

  1. Wait for the chat launcher to appear.
  2. Open the widget and send a test message.
  3. Confirm that the conversation reaches the expected Webex Connect flow or agent.

No chat launcher?

Check that the widget is enabled and this site's domain is allowed in your Webex Connect configuration. Browser privacy settings, content blockers, and agent availability may also affect the widget.

For testing only. Do not send passwords, payment information, or other sensitive data. Chat messages are handled by your configured Webex service.
